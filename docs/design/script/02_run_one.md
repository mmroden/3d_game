# Script 02 — Run One and the Erosion Arc (register draft)

Built from Mark's notes of 2026-08-30 and nothing else. Everything here is
disposable — redline freely. Lines and ideas marked ⚠ are my derivations
from those notes, not things the notes say; they are offers, collected in
the redline list at the bottom.

**Cast: two voices, both performed by Mark.**

- **CONTROL** — the man in the chair. A military scout-flight control
  tower: jargon-dense and straight-laced at first, increasingly off-book
  as the repetition sets in and the befuddlement mounts. He sees what the
  Player sees. He cannot hear what the Player says — the Other has cut
  that path.
- **THE OTHER** — the computer the Player is unknowingly corresponding
  with. Garbled beyond comprehension early; un-garbles as the Player gets
  further. Recorded clean, garbled in post — so when the voice finally
  clears, it is unmistakably the same voice as Control. It always was.

The Player talks; we never hear it, and neither does Control. `[CALLSIGN]`
is a placeholder for Mark to fill. No other names exist anywhere —
"Control" is a radio address.

## The shape

- **See, don't hear.** Every Control line is half a conversation: orders,
  narration, guesses — never confirmation. He can watch the Player comply,
  fail, hesitate; he can never be told why.
- ⚠ **The interception.** The Player's outbound signal doesn't die — it
  *arrives*, at the Other, the thing that blocked it. Every call the
  Player keys toward base is received by the computer alone. That is the
  unknowing correspondence: the Player has been talking to it since the
  first radio check, and its half of the conversation starts as garbage.
- **What the garble is made of.** The Other's early vocabulary is the
  channel's most-repeated material — radio checks, the RTB order, the
  callsign — because that is all it has ever heard. Its infancy is literal
  repetition: it can only say what the wire says most. Between Control
  noticing the loops and the Other being *built* out of them, the game's
  repetitiveness is voiced by the entire cast.
- **Erosion ladder.** Planet 1: by the book. Planet 2: procedure fraying,
  repetition noticed. Planet 3: off-book — befuddlement peak among the
  apartments, schools, libraries. Planet 4: whatever is left of him, over
  an actual asteroid surface, a drone screen, and the last meteor launcher
  still aimed at Earth.
- **Craft rule:** the erosion is countable. Procedure words per line
  (*copy, stand by, be advised, as fragged, over*) fall planet by planet.
  If a late line sounds crisp, it had better be crisp on purpose.

## Planet 1 — by the book

Cold open, first boot:

> **[C-01]** "[CALLSIGN], Control. Radio check, over." *(3–4s of nothing)*
> "Negative copy. …Feed is five-by — I have your eyes, no voice. Roll
> ninety and back if you read me." *(the Player moves)* "Good roll. Okay.
> We work with what we've got."
> *VA: tower-crisp; a workaround invented without drama. The sortie is
> already wrong and he is already coping.*

> **[C-02]** "Be advised, your transmit path shows green on my board.
> Green, and I receive nothing. Logging it for the techs and moving on."
> *⚠ seeds the interception: the signal goes somewhere.*

> **[C-03]** "Tasking as fragged: scout sweep of the anomaly volume,
> waypoints alpha through delta. Sensors hot, weapons cold. Map it, tag
> it, come home. Simple day."
> *VA: no irony on "simple day." The irony accrues to it later — refrain
> candidate.* ⚠

First hostile contact:

> **[C-04]** "Contact — your twelve, closing. That's nothing of ours.
> Weapons free, [CALLSIGN]. Weapons free."
> *VA: his own "weapons cold" shredded in minute five; he hears it happen.*

> **[C-05]** *(first kill)* "Splash one. …This was supposed to be a
> survey."

Geometry wrongness:

> **[C-06]** "Your chart and mine disagree. There is no interior volume at
> this fix. You are flying through something that is not on anything."
> *VA: instruments read aloud; editorializing refused.*

The RTB thread begins:

> **[C-07]** "That's enough for one day. Abort tasking. RTB, best speed —
> we sort the charts on the ground."

> **[C-08]** *(the order fails — no route home exists)* "…You're outbound.
> Either you can't hear the order or you can't fly it. I can see you,
> [CALLSIGN]. I just can't tell which it is."
> *VA: the register drops half a step. First personal sentence in the
> piece.*

> **[C-09]** *(watching a return attempt fail)* "I watched you fly the
> return leg. Twice. The picture doesn't change. …Stand by. Thinking."
> *VA: "stand by, thinking" is not a procedure call. That is the first
> crack.*

⚠ Deaths as Control sees them (feed cut, then the start grid again):

> **[C-10]** "Feed's gone— …Feed's back. Same compartment."
> *VA: flat; refusing to react is the reaction.*

> **[C-11]** "Lost the picture. Got the picture. In that order. Keep it in
> that order."

> **[C-12]** "You're back on the start grid. I won't pretend I understand
> that. Sweep continues."

> **[C-13]** "Feed cut for eleven seconds. Felt longer from this chair."

Idle:

> **[C-14]** "Control's up. No traffic."

> **[C-15]** "Holding. Your pace."

End of the first session:

> **[C-16]** "We'll call that sortie one. Debrief would be good. Debrief
> needs your voice. …File what you can, wherever you're filing it."
> *VA: talking to himself by the last clause.*

The Other, planet 1 — two placements, both nearly nothing:

> **[O-01]** *(late in the run, shortly after an RTB call, arriving where
> base's answer should live)* heard as: "—(squelch)— …—urn— …—turn—"
> *The true line underneath is the RTB order coming back. Its first words
> are the wire's most-repeated words. The Player should file this as base
> breaking through interference. It is not base.* ⚠

> **[O-02]** *(after an early death)* heard as: "—say— —gain—"
> *("Say again" — the second-most-common phrase on a channel nobody
> answers.)*

## Planet 2 — procedure fraying (samples)

> **[C-20]** "Same compartment classes as yesterday. Different order.
> Decks don't reshuffle. Noting that they reshuffled."

> **[C-21]** "Command acknowledged my log. Same acknowledgment as last
> time. Word for word. I compared." ⚠

> **[C-22]** "Sortie eleven, same tasking. I've stopped reading the
> tasking. It doesn't change, and neither does anything else out here."

> **[C-23]** "RTB when able. …That's me saying it for the record. We both
> know how it goes now."
> *VA: the order as ritual — he says it so somebody said it.*

> **[C-24]** "Your transmit path is still green. Fourteen days of green
> and silence. Somebody is getting your traffic, [CALLSIGN], and it is not
> me."
> *⚠ the interception said out loud — he works it out and can do nothing
> with it. VA: troubleshooting tone, not paranoia. Worse that way.*

> **[O-10]** heard as: "Return to base. Return— to base. Return."
> *Whole phrase now; stress slightly off; played where no base exists. The
> wrongness is placement, not wording.*

> **[O-11]** heard as: "[CALLSIGN]."
> *One word, nearly clean — and no channel hiss under it. If the Player
> notices anything, it is that this one didn't crackle.*

## Planet 3 — befuddlement peak

> **[C-30]** "New volume. Stand by, descript— …It's a hallway. Apartment
> block. Doors, numbers, a mat outside one of them."
> *VA: procedure fighting the content and losing.*

> **[C-31]** "That's a school. Corridors, lockers, chairs for small
> people. My rangefinder puts us a very long way from anywhere a school
> has business being."

> **[C-32]** "Library. Reading room, long tables, little lamps. The spines
> render. The titles don't. …I've been staring at that sentence in my log
> for a while now."

> **[C-33]** "Somebody set a table in there. Two places. *(long pause; the
> mic stays open)* Continue sweep."
> *VA: the pause is the line; "continue sweep" is where he goes to hide.*

> **[C-34]** "Nine years flying scout. I've called in wrecks, mines, one
> memorable ice calving. Never once a kitchen."
> *VA: almost laughing. Not laughing.*

> **[C-35]** "Ask why you're not home yet, they say. Ask HOW, I say.
> [CALLSIGN], I have been asking how since sortie one, and I don't think
> anyone is getting my traffic either."
> *VA: fully off-book. The straight-laced frame is just gone.*

The Other, nearly clear:

> **[O-20]** ⚠ "Return to base. This is base."
> *Four words that decide a lot — offered, not locked. It has begun
> answering the Player's calls* as *base. VA: identical timbre to Control
> now; the only tell left is a channel too clean.*

> **[O-21]** "Say again. …I read you."

## Planet 4 — sketch only

The board per Mark: an actual asteroid surface; drones protecting the last
meteor launcher, still aimed at Earth. Three register samples and nothing
more — the plotting here is Mark's to call:

> **[C-40]** "Hard structure on the long horizon. Rail-straight,
> kilometers of it. …That's a launcher, [CALLSIGN]. And the bore is laid
> on home."
> *VA: the jargon comes back for one line — some things need procedure to
> be sayable at all.*

> **[C-41]** "Drone screen between you and it. They're not scrambling —
> they were already up. Standing patrol. Someone expected company,
> someday."

> **[O-30]** *(clean)* "Radio check. …I read you five by five. I have
> always read you."
> *The whole conceit in three sentences, in Control's voice, on a channel
> with no hiss in it.*
